Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. This policy covers the Vicus apps for iPhone and Apple Watch.

The short version: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not receive your data. Your workouts, routes, calendar and health data stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch and in Apple Health. To plan a route and show the weather, the app asks outside map and weather services about the area around your start; those requests carry that location and nothing that says who you are.

What stays on your devices

  • Health and fitness data. With your permission Vicus reads workouts, heart rate and related measurements from Apple Health to build and adjust your plan and to describe each session afterwards. It saves the workouts you record with Vicus, with their routes, to Apple Health. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Location. Vicus uses your location to start routes where you are and to follow and record a workout. The recorded route is saved to Apple Health on your devices.
  • Calendar. With your permission Vicus reads your calendar on the iPhone to find time for sessions, and writes the sessions you accept to a calendar of its own. Calendar contents are not sent anywhere.
  • Your answers and preferences. What you tell Vicus about your goal, your health and what you liked or changed stays in the app on your iPhone.
  • The words after a workout. Every number in a debrief is computed on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model may put those numbers into sentences; that happens on the iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

Vicus has no servers of its own that receive your data. It makes these requests to services run by others:

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
OpenStreetMap data servers (the Overpass API at overpass-api.de and overpass.kumi.systems)The corners of a box of streets around where your route startsTo get the street map that routes are planned from, on your iPhone
Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com)Your start location rounded to about one kilometreThe forecast and air quality for your sessions
Apple MapsThe area of the map you are looking atTo draw the map
Hugging Face (huggingface.co)A one-time download request, with no information about youTo fetch the voice the coach speaks in

As with any internet request, these services see the IP address the request comes from. The requests do not include your name, an account, an advertising identifier or your health data. Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

If you open a link to directions in another maps app, that app receives the places in the link.

Logs you choose to send

Settings has a Send logs button. It makes a file of what Vicus said and did in your recent workouts, including the coaching lines with the heart rates and paces spoken in them, and opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you send it. If you email it to us for help, we use it to look into your problem and for nothing else.

What we do not do

  • No account, and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or advertising software from other companies in the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not receive it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data is on your devices, you control it. Deleting Vicus removes the data it keeps. Workouts saved to Apple Health remain until you delete them in the Health app. You can withdraw Health, Location or Calendar access at any time in Settings on your iPhone.

Who Vicus is for

Vicus is for people aged 13 and over, and is rated 13+ on the App Store. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, this page changes first and the date above is updated.
