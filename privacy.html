Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This policy covers the Vicus apps for iPhone and Apple Watch.

The short version: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not receive your data unless you choose to share anonymous usage counts (off by default). Your workouts, routes, calendar and health data stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch and in Apple Health. To plan a route and show the weather, the app asks outside map and weather services about the area around your start; those requests carry that location and nothing that says who you are.

What stays on your devices

  • Health and fitness data. With your permission Vicus reads workouts, heart rate and related measurements from Apple Health to build and adjust your plan and to describe each session afterwards. It saves the workouts you record with Vicus, with their routes, to Apple Health. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Location. Vicus uses your location to start routes where you are and to follow and record a workout. The recorded route is saved to Apple Health on your devices.
  • Calendar. With your permission Vicus reads your calendar on the iPhone to find time for sessions, and writes the sessions you accept to a calendar of its own. Calendar contents are not sent anywhere.
  • Your answers and preferences. What you tell Vicus about your goal, your health and what you liked or changed stays in the app on your iPhone.
  • The words after a workout. Every number in a debrief is computed on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model may put those numbers into sentences; that happens on the iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

Vicus has no servers of its own that receive your data. It makes these requests to services run by others:

As with any internet request, these services see the IP address the request comes from. The requests do not include your name, an account, an advertising identifier or your health data. Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

If you open a link to directions in another maps app, that app receives the places in the link.

Anonymous usage sharing (optional, off by default)

Settings has a switch, Share anonymous usage. It is off until you turn it on, and Vicus works the same either way. While it is off, nothing described in this section is made, stored or sent. When it is on, Vicus sends a few counts about how the app is used so we can see what helps and what does not. They go to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH), a privacy-focused analytics service that processes them on our behalf and may not use them for anything else.

What is sent. Only these seven events, with only these fields:

app_opened_weekOnce a week, when you open the appHow many weeks since your first launch

Every event also carries the app build number, whether it came from the iPhone or the Apple Watch, and a random install id. The install id is made on your iPhone when you turn sharing on. It is not your Apple ID, your email or anything from Health, and it cannot be traced back to you. TelemetryDeck hashes it again before storing it. Like any internet request, the request reaches TelemetryDeck from your IP address; TelemetryDeck does not keep it.

What is never sent. Your location or any place, routes or their shape, heart rate or any other health data, calendar contents, anything you type, your name or email, an advertising identifier, or the contents of your workouts. The events above are the whole list; anything not on it is dropped on your iPhone before it is sent.

Turning it off. Switch off Share anonymous usage in Settings at any time. Vicus stops sending, throws away the install id and the weekly tally it kept on your iPhone, and a new random id is made if you turn it on again. Because the id is random and not linked to you, we cannot find or delete the events of one person; they are removed when they age out.

How long we keep it. Events are kept for 13 months and then deleted. That period is our own choice, not one TelemetryDeck sets. The counts are used only to improve Vicus, never for advertising or tracking.

Logs you choose to send

Settings has a Send logs button. It makes a file of what Vicus said and did in your recent workouts, including the coaching lines with the heart rates and paces spoken in them, and opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you send it. If you email it to us for help, we use it to look into your problem and for nothing else.

What we do not do

  • No account, and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No advertising software from other companies in the app. The only analytics is the optional, off-by-default usage sharing above.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not receive it. The anonymous counts you may choose to share are not linked to you and are used only to improve Vicus.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data is on your devices, you control it. Deleting Vicus removes the data it keeps. Workouts saved to Apple Health remain until you delete them in the Health app. You can withdraw Health, Location or Calendar access at any time in Settings on your iPhone.

Who Vicus is for

Vicus is for people aged 13 and over, and is rated 13+ on the App Store. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, this page changes first and the date above is updated.
