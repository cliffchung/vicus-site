Support

Something not working, or a question about your plan? Write to us and a person will answer.

Email vicuscoach@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone and Apple Watch you have, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Sending logs

If we ask for logs: in Vicus on your iPhone, open Settings and tap Send logs. It makes one file of what Vicus said and did in your recent workouts, on the iPhone and the watch, and opens the share sheet so you can attach it to your email. The file includes the coaching lines, with the heart rates and paces spoken in them. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Common questions

Vicus asks for Health access. What does it use?

It reads your past workouts, heart rate and related measurements to set your starting fitness and shape your plan, and it saves the workouts you do with Vicus to Apple Health. That data stays on your devices. You can change what Vicus may read in the Health app, under Sharing.

My music pauses or dips when the coach speaks

When your headphones are connected to your iPhone, the iPhone speaks the coaching over your music at a lower volume and the music comes back straight after. If a line is spoken by the watch instead, your headphones may switch to the watch for a moment.

My plan did not reach my watch

Open Vicus on the iPhone with the watch nearby and unlocked; the plan is sent again by itself. If the watch app is not installed, install it from the Watch app on your iPhone.

How do I change or skip a session?

On Today, use Change or Can't make it. Vicus suggests how the rest of the week should move and waits for you to accept.

How do I delete my data?

Vicus keeps its data on your iPhone and watch. Deleting the app removes it. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes. Vicus is for people aged 13 and over.

Is Vicus a medical device?

No. It is a training tool. If something hurts or you feel unwell, stop, and talk to a doctor before you train again.
