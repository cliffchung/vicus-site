Vicus

Your neighbourhood is the gym

Vicus plans runs, rides, walks and strength around your week, picks routes from your door, and coaches you through each session on iPhone and Apple Watch.

A route for every workout

Quiet streets, safer crossings, the distance your plan needs. Vicus learns what you like from your feedback.

A plan that fits your week

Tell Vicus your goal and it reads your calendar to find the days that work. Miss a day and it suggests a change for you to accept; it never rewrites what you were shown.

Private by design

There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Your workouts, routes and health data stay on your iPhone and in Apple Health. The privacy policy says exactly what leaves your phone and why.
